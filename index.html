Theory

Mathematical Model

Total Daily Energy E = ∑ i = 1 24 P i Δ t
Average Load P avg = E T
Load Factor LF = P avg P max × 100 %
Load Duration Curve P LDC = sort ( P 1 , P 2 , … , P 24 )
Peak Demand P max = max ( P 1 , … , P 24 )

For this simulation, each step has a duration of 1 hour. Therefore, the area under the chronological load curve directly represents daily energy.
